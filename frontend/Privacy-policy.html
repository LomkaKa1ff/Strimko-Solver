Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Strimko Solver is a personal project. We respect your privacy and process only the minimum amount of data required to solve puzzles.

2. Data Collection

We process images you upload for puzzle solving. Images are processed temporarily and are deleted automatically in 48 hours after the analysis is complete. We do not store your personal photos.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve our Strimko puzzle-solving service, analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience and respond to your inquiries or feedback

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

5. Cookies

We may use basic technical cookies to remember your grid size preferences.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

Questions? Contact us through GitHub Issues. Issues are public; do not include personal information.
