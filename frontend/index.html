How to Use?

Manually

  • Click on a circle to enter a number.
  • Shift + Click to connect chains.
  • Click Solve to finish.

By Screenshot

Simply upload your image, and the solver will identify the numbers and streams automatically.

What is Strimko?

Strimko is a logic number puzzle based on rows, columns, and streams. Each must contain unique numbers from 1 to N.
